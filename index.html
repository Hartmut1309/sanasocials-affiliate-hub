Compliance

The rules that keep everyone's account alive

We run long-term offers in regulated health categories. These rules are enforced without exceptions — violations mean immediate, permanent blacklisting across all our offers. Before you write a single ad: the pre-approved claims center lists what you may say per offer — and rule 1 below is absolute.

  1. The retired name. “ProNutraQuest” is withdrawn and must not be used — remove all existing material. It must not appear anywhere in your promotion, in text, image, video or audio: ad copy and creatives, thumbnails and video frames, pre-sells and landing pages, email subject lines, from-names and sending domains, your own domains and social handles, and paid-search keywords and bids — on Meta, Google/YouTube, email and every other channel — in any spelling, translation or lookalike form. Already-published, archived, scheduled and automated material counts: delete the old files rather than just stopping. You are responsible for your sub-affiliates, agencies and any traffic you buy. Any use is a material breach and grounds for immediate termination. Promote only under the individual product names shown on each card.
  2. No spam. Ever. Email traffic must be CAN-SPAM / GDPR compliant, with working unsubscribe and honest sender identity.
  3. No deceptive claims. Nothing misleading, untruthful or unsubstantiated. Use the claims in our official materials — do not invent cure promises, disease claims or guaranteed results.
  4. No fake authority. No fabricated testimonials, no before/after photos without written permission, no deepfakes or celebrity endorsements, no fake news formats.
  5. Don't impersonate the brand. No brand-bidding on our product names in paid search without written approval, and never present yourself as the product owner.
  6. Send traffic to our funnels. No direct-linking to checkout that bypasses the offer presentation, and no selling on Amazon, eBay or other retail platforms.
  7. Disclose your affiliation. FTC/consumer-law compliant disclosure on review pages, ads and emails, in the local language of your traffic.
  8. Respect the customer. No "scam"-style negative advertising against our or competitor products, and no retargeting scraped from our pixels.
  9. When unsure, ask first. One email to support beats a burned account. Gray-area channels (Telegram blasts, incentivized traffic, adult) need written approval.
