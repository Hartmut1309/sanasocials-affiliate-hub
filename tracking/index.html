Tracking Setup Guide

Attribution that survives scale

Lost attribution is the quiet scaling killer: the campaign works, the dashboard says it doesn't, and a winner gets killed. Ten minutes of setup before the first euro of spend prevents all of it.

1 · Campaign keys — your only clean read

Every link on the hub carries ?aff=your-id&cam=campaign-key. The cam key flows into Digistore24 reporting, so it is the one label that follows a sale all the way to commission. Use one key per creative or ad set you will ever want to read separately.

Traffic sourceKey patternExample
Meta / Facebookmeta-{angle}-{adset}meta-sludge-b3
Native (Taboola, Outbrain…)ntv-{network}-{widget}ntv-taboola-w41
YouTubeyt-{angle}-{cut}yt-locks-cut3
Emailem-{list}-{send}em-health-aug12

Rules that keep the data readable: lowercase, hyphens only, never reuse a key across channels, and agree the convention with Stefan before launch so our reporting and yours name the same thing the same way.

2 · Pixel & server-to-server postback

All nine funnels support the Digistore24 tracking pixel and S2S postbacks, configured from your own Digistore24 account. The exact, always-current setup steps live in the official Digistore24 help center — search "tracking pixel" or "postback". Tell Stefan which platform you optimize on (Meta CAPI, native network S2S, etc.) and he confirms the event mapping on our side before you spend.

3 · Delayed CTAs on VSL pages

Our VSL buy buttons appear mid-video, not on page load. For young campaigns that means early micro-events (initiate-checkout and player engagement) are the honest early signal; switch optimization to purchase events once volume supports it. The volume-operator form on the hub asks for exactly this optimization model — that's why.

4 · Running your own pre-sell into EU traffic?

If you put a quiz, listicle or advertorial in front of our funnel, GDPR applies to your page: non-essential pixels need a consent banner before they fire. Our funnel pages handle their own consent — your pre-sell needs its own. Budget for it; consent-loss is measurement-loss, not a reason to skip the banner.

5 · The pre-spend checklist

  • Digistore24 ID entered on the hub — every link personalized
  • Campaign-key convention agreed with Stefan (one key per creative/ad set)
  • Pixel or postback test conversion confirmed in your DS24 account
  • Language German, geo DE + AT + CH first — expand once the funnel proves
