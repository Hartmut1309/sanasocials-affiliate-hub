What you may say — and what gets accounts banned

We run long-term offers in a regulated health category. The upside: you never have to guess. Everything below is safe to run; everything in the red box ends the partnership. When a claim isn't listed, ask before you launch — one email beats a burned ad account.

✓ Approved for every offer

  • The 60-day money-back guarantee — all offers, every funnel.
  • The doctor credential stack: "a genuinely reputable, well-known doctor — Harvard-trained, 17+ years in practice, featured on ABC, NBC and FOX." His name is deliberately not used in affiliate marketing — run the credentials, not a name. It's the single-doctor model (think Dr. Gundry) replicated for DACH, and it's the trust edge typical Digistore24/ClickBank offers never have.
  • Every headline, mechanism and hook line published on the offer cards and dedicated affiliate pages — approved to reuse verbatim in your ads, pre-sells and emails.
  • "Native German-built funnels — not translated US pages."
  • Program facts as published: RevShare 60–75% through the entire funnel (thank-you page included), CPA €120–150 (currently $137–171), published EPCs/AOVs with their dates.

✗ Never — on any surface, in any language

  • Disease claims: cure, treat, heal, prevent, or reverse any disease or medical condition.
  • Guaranteed results — health outcomes or earnings.
  • Before/after photos without our written permission.
  • Fabricated testimonials, fake experts, deepfakes, celebrity endorsements, fake-news formats.
  • Inventing study citations or inflating the documented science.
  • Presenting supplement information as medical advice — consumer-facing pages carry the standard supplement disclaimer.
